I partner with scientists and biotech teams to turn complex mechanisms and data into visuals that communicate clearly — whether that's a publication figure, a pitch deck, an investor brief, or an outreach campaign.

---

### How it works

**1. Brief**
We start with a short conversation about your project: the science, the audience, and what you need the visual to do. No jargon, no lengthy forms — just a chat.

**2. Concept**
I put together an initial sketch or style direction based on your brief. You give feedback, we align on the approach before any detailed work starts.

**3. Illustration**
I develop the final artwork in rounds, sharing progress along the way. You stay in control of the direction; I handle the craft.

**4. Delivery**
You receive production-ready files in the formats you need — print, web, presentation, or all three. Source files included.
